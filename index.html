How the hunt works

🎯

Filter the whole tape

Exchange, price and volume, twelve fundamental metrics and thirteen technical indicators — every filter is optional, and they stack.

🐍

Rank with the Snake Score

Technical, momentum, volume and fundamental sub-scores combine into one 0–100 number, so the strongest matches sort to the top by themselves.

👁

Keep an eye on the target

Open any symbol for charts and the full indicator set, then save it to your watchlist — stored in your browser, no account needed.

Demo data. This build runs on a generated universe of fictional tickers so the scanner can be developed before a market data provider is connected. The Snake Score measures how well a stock fits the selected criteria — it is not a price forecast and not investment advice.
